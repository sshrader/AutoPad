    How it works

    1. LaunchName your coin, add a picture, and launch it on pump.fun. Optionally buy some in the same transaction, so you are the first buyer.
    2. Choose your optionsPick who gets the creator fees, and add a boost or an airdrop if you want one. These are fixed when the coin is created.
    3. TradeEvery buy and sell on pump.fun adds creator fees. They build up in the coin's fee vault.
    4. Get paidThe AutoPay keeper checks every few minutes and sends the waiting fees to everyone in the split. No claiming.

    Launch options

    Creator rewards

    The default. You keep 90% of creator fees and {{name}} takes 10%. Paid to your wallet automatically.

    Share with a wallet

    Split your share with a partner, a friend, a dev or a charity: name their wallet and give them up to 40%. {{name}} still takes 10%, and you keep the rest.

    Holder rewards Coming soon

    All creator fees go to the coin's holders through pump.fun. A flat launch fee of 0.05 SOL applies, and there is no first buy.

    Boost

    Pay a flat fee to put your coin in the Promoted row on this page for a set time. Longer boosts cost more in total and less per hour. The highest SOL per hour gets the top spot. Paid placement is always labeled, and the fee is not refunded.

      Auto airdrop

      Set aside part of your first buy for holders. When enough wallets hold your coin and the wait is over, the keeper splits those tokens between them. Your tokens stay in your wallet until then, so this is not a lock: sell them or cancel the permission and nothing is sent.

      The fee split, boost and airdrop are all checked by the server and written into your launch transaction. {{name}} never holds your fees or your tokens.

      Every trade pays a creator fee, shared on-chain between the wallets in each coin's split. AutoPay pays it out for you, and a payout can only go to those wallets.
