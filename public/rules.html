How payouts work

{{name}} sends your creator fees to your wallet for you. Here is exactly what it does, and what it cannot do.

What happens

  • The split is fixed at launch. When a coin is created, its fee split is written on-chain with pump.fun: 90% to the wallet that launched it and 10% to {{name}}. Nobody can change it afterwards.
  • A keeper checks every coin. A small server job looks at each coin's waiting creator fees on a schedule. You can see when the next check is on the home page.
  • It pays when it is worth it. A coin is paid out once it has enough fees waiting to be worth the network fee. The amount is shown on the home page's stub.
  • Every payout is public. Each one is a normal Solana transaction. The latest ones are listed on the home page, with a link to the explorer.

Launch options

  • Creator rewards. The default: you keep 90% of creator fees and {{name}} takes 10%, paid out automatically.
  • Share with a wallet. Name a partner wallet and a whole-number share (up to 40%). {{name}} still takes 10% and you keep the rest. Fixed when the coin is created.
  • Holder rewards. All creator fees go to the coin's holders through pump.fun, for a flat launch fee and with no first buy. Switched on only once it has been tested.
  • Boost. A flat fee, paid inside your launch transaction, puts your coin in the labeled Promoted row on the home page. The price follows a curve: longer boosts cost more in total and less per hour, and you can enter your own amount. The row is ranked by SOL paid per hour, there are a few slots, and fees are not refunded. The server checks on-chain that the fee was paid before the boost starts. The site's own first coin, launched from the admin page, is the flagship and stays in the row for free.
  • Auto airdrop. Set aside part of your first buy for holders. Your launch asks your wallet to let the keeper send those tokens later, and once both the wait and the holder count are met, it splits them between holders by how much each holds. The tokens stay in your wallet until then: this is not a lock, and if you sell them or cancel the permission, nothing is sent. Only offered when the site has switched it on.

What the keeper cannot do

It never holds your fees. pump.fun's payout can only move fees to the wallets already in the coin's split, no matter who sends it, so the keeper cannot send them anywhere else. The keeper's own wallet only holds a few cents of SOL to pay network fees. For an airdrop, it can only move the tokens you approved, and only to holders.

If you would rather not wait

The wallet that launched a coin sees a "Pay out now" button on the coin's page. It does the same payout straight away, to the same wallets.

Good to know

Coins that have moved to their regular trading pool are paid out on pump.fun, not here. If the keeper is switched off or out of SOL for network fees, the home page says so, and fees keep building up safely until it is back. Coins are made by users and can lose all their value.
