How it works.

1. You launch a coin

Choose a name, ticker and picture, then approve one transaction from your wallet. It creates the coin on Meteora’s bonding curve and, if you ask for it, makes your first buy in the same transaction.

2. People trade on the curve

Anyone can buy and sell on the curve. Every trade pays a small fee in SOL. That fee is set when the curve is made. For the first few seconds after a coin launches the fee is very high (starting at 50%) to put off bots, then it settles at 3.5%.

3. Fees are claimed for you

The fees from your coin build up on chain. On a schedule, the AutoPayment claimer collects the creator’s share and sends it straight to the wallet you launched with. You do not have to claim anything. The platform’s share is collected the same way and sent to the platform wallet.

Who holds the money?

Nobody holds it for you. The claimer wallet signs the claim, but the payout goes directly to your wallet in the same transaction. Every payout is on chain and listed on the coin page.

What if the claimer stops?

Your fees stay on chain and are not lost. They are paid out the next time the claimer runs.

Graduation

When enough SOL has been raised, the coin graduates and its liquidity moves to a regular Meteora pool. Trading then continues there, outside this site, and liquidity is locked.

The flagship coin
